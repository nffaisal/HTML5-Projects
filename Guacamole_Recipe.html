Guacamole Recipe

Instructions

  1. Avocado
  2. Red onion
  3. cilantro
  4. Jalepeno
  5. minced Garlic
  6. Fresh Lime Juice
  7. Salt
  8. Pepper

Ingredients

  • peel avocdo and remove innards into a bowl.
  • mash avocado with a fork until smooth.
  • cut jalapeno into small peices and add to the bowl.
  • add a tablespoon of lime juice.
  • add salt and pepper.
  • ix to incorporate.
  • Top with chopped Cilantro.

Quantity

Ingredients Amount
avocado 2 large
salt 1/4 tsp
Pepper 1/4 tsp
